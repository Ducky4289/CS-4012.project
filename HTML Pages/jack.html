Dublin

How to get there

By plane

Fly to Dublin Airport, then take an airport bus or taxi into the city.

By ferry

Ferries arrive at Dublin Port. Continue into the city by bus or taxi.

By train or coach

Trains from elsewhere in Ireland arrive at Heuston or Connolly Station. Coaches also serve the city.

Where to stay

Hotel

Look in the city centre or around St Stephen's Green.

Hostel

Look around Temple Bar or Gardiner Street.

Camping

Camac Valley Caravan & Camping Park is in Clondalkin. Visit its website. Check its current renovation closure notice before planning a stay.

Paris

How to get there

By plane

Fly to Charles de Gaulle or Orly Airport, then continue into Paris by public transport or taxi.

By train

Trains arrive at one of Paris's main stations, which connect to the Metro.

By coach

Long-distance coaches travel to Paris from cities around France and Europe.

Where to stay

Hotel

Look near the Louvre or in the 7th arrondissement.

Hostel

Look around the Latin Quarter or Bastille.

Camping

Camping de Paris is in the Bois de Boulogne. Visit its website.

Rome

How to get there

By plane

Fly to Rome Fiumicino or Ciampino Airport, then continue into the city by train, bus, or taxi.

By train

Trains from other Italian cities arrive at stations such as Roma Termini.

By coach

Long-distance coaches connect Rome with other Italian cities. Check where your coach arrives.

Where to stay

Hotel

Look in the historic centre or near Roma Termini station.

Hostel

Look around Monti or near Roma Termini.

Camping

Seven Hills Camping & Village is in Rome's Veio Park. Visit its website.

Barcelona

How to get there

By plane

Fly to Barcelona–El Prat Airport, then continue into the city by train, bus, Metro, or taxi.

By train

Barcelona Sants has train connections with cities in Spain and France.

By coach or ferry

Coaches arrive from cities around Europe and Spain. Ferries connect Barcelona with destinations including the Balearic Islands.

Where to stay

Hotel

Look in Eixample or near the old town.

Hostel

Look around the Gothic Quarter or El Born.

Camping

Camping 3 Estrellas is in Gavà, south of Barcelona, beside the beach. Visit its website.

Tokyo

How to get there

By plane

Fly to Haneda or Narita Airport, then continue into Tokyo by train or airport bus.

By bullet train

If travelling from another Japanese city, take the Shinkansen to Tokyo.

By bus or ferry

Long-distance buses and ferries also connect Tokyo with other parts of Japan.

Where to stay

Hotel

Look in Shinjuku or near Tokyo Station.

Hostel

Look around Asakusa or Ueno.

Camping

Jonanjima Seaside Park Campground is beside Tokyo Bay. Reservations are required. Visit the campground page.

New York

How to get there

By plane

Fly to JFK, LaGuardia, or Newark Airport, then continue into the city by public transport or taxi.

By train

Amtrak trains arrive at Moynihan Train Hall, next to Penn Station in Manhattan.

By coach

Coaches arrive at the Port Authority Bus Terminal in Manhattan.

Where to stay

Hotel

Look in Midtown Manhattan, near Penn Station.

Hostel

Look in Brooklyn or near a subway station in Manhattan.

Camping

Camp Gateway is at Floyd Bennett Field in Brooklyn. It is seasonal, so check its dates before planning a stay. Visit the campground page.
