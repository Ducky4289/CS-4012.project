Explore Destinations

Explore some popular places to visit.

Spain

Spain is known for its sunny beaches, football and delicious food. You can visit Barcelona or enjoy a holiday by the sea.

Capital: Madrid

Egypt

Egypt is famous for its ancient pyramids, deserts and the River Nile. The Red Sea is also a great place for diving.

Capital: Cairo

Italy

Italy is a great place to enjoy pizza, pasta and historic buildings. Visit the Colosseum in Rome or the canals of Venice.

Capital: Rome
